Problem
The vector finder returned 17x25: eleven printed body bands each contained
five jurisdiction records, one header tier was collapsed, and the trailing
notes/disclaimers were included as full-width cells. Two empty body columns
were real printed fields and must not be deleted.

Correction
The repair now recognizes repeated compact records across at least three
consecutive ruled bands. Each band must have consistent field occupancy
across most columns, predominantly single-token aligned fields and regular
line spacing. Original columns, including empty ones, remain authoritative.
A single wrapped row or paragraph cannot establish this pattern.

Header tier recovery additionally requires physical lower-tier vertical
segments at every needed subdivision and no word crossing the tier boundary.
Terminal-note exclusion additionally requires a complete single-line data
row, an empty spacer band, and a final full-width prose block whose words
cross the table's established column positions. Ordinary summary rows,
populated spacers and later separate-column cells prevent exclusion.
All results remain unreviewed and semantic_structure_verified:false.
