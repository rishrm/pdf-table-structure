What changed
1. A supporting horizontal rule must lie within the candidate's vertical
   extent (two-point tolerance), overlap at least 75% of its width, measure
   at least 12 points (the existing pixel-rule minimum), and be no more than
   1.5 times its width. Nearby running headers, map axes and tiny texture
   strokes no longer qualify merely because they overlap horizontally.
2. Word crossings are checked separately for each actual cell divider.
   At least three cut rows covering at least half its observed rows reject
   the candidate. Merged-cell interiors do not count as dividers. This closes
   the case where a wide grid dilutes repeated cuts below the global limit.
3. Wordy unruled fields separated by ordinary interword-sized gaps are
   rejected when at least one field lacks recurring left, right or center
   alignment. Compact numeric fields, aligned multiword fields and supported
   printed-rule layouts have positive regression controls.
