Fix
Some documents paint a cell background and then repaint smaller rectangles
behind its text. Treating all those invisible repaint edges as table borders
can produce extra columns, overlapping cells or a missing table.
Vector table finding now ignores a fill-only rectangle strictly contained in
a larger same-color fill. Strokes, thin rules, different/unknown colors,
partial overlaps and any intersecting drawing are protected. Cleanup is
bounded to 1,000 rectangles and 6,000 other drawings; failure or budget exhaustion
retains the original vector route. Source PDFs/rendering are not modified.
